Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dolphin Group ("Dolphin Group," "we," "us," or "our"), an independent software studio based in Alberta, Canada, handles information in Idle Foods Merger and any other application, website, feature, or service that links to this policy (collectively, the "Services").

Current game saves are local. Idle Foods Merger currently stores game progress and settings in private storage on the player's device. Dolphin Group does not currently operate an account or cloud-save server for the game.

Information we handle

Game and app information

The Services may process game progress, settings, preferences, achievements, in-game balances, reward and advertising events, app version, and similar operational information. In Idle Foods Merger, save data currently remains on the device unless the player uses a device or operating-system feature outside our control to move it.

Advertising, consent, device, and diagnostic information

We use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google and its partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and general location inferred from it;
  • device, app-set, advertising, and account-related identifiers;
  • app launches, taps, ad requests, ad impressions, video views, rewarded-ad events, and other interactions;
  • device, browser, operating-system, network, language, and app information;
  • crash, performance, energy-use, and other diagnostic information; and
  • privacy choices and consent signals.

Google may use this information for advertising, ad personalization where permitted, analytics, measurement, service delivery, security, and fraud prevention. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Information you choose to provide

We may receive information you voluntarily provide when you contact us or use an available account, cloud synchronization, support, survey, promotion, community, or similar feature. Depending on the feature, this may include an email address, account identifier, game progress, settings, purchase or reward records, support messages, uploaded content, and other information you choose to submit.

How we use information

We may use information to:

  • provide, save, synchronize, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • deliver and verify advertising and in-app rewards;
  • personalize content or advertising where permitted;
  • measure usage, diagnose problems, and develop features;
  • protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, and enforce our terms;
  • respond to support, privacy, and other communications; and
  • meet legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and security obligations.

When information is shared

We may share information with service providers that perform services for us, including advertising, consent management, hosting, analytics, diagnostics, communications, security, and cloud infrastructure. Google acts as an advertising and consent-management provider for Idle Foods Merger.

We may also disclose information when required by law; to protect rights, safety, and security; in connection with a merger, financing, sale, reorganization, or transfer of all or part of our business; or with your direction or consent. We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws may define personalized advertising as a "sale" or "sharing"; where applicable, users can exercise available choices through the app's privacy controls or device advertising settings.

Consent and advertising choices

Where required, the Services request advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Eligible users can revisit those choices through the app's Privacy choices control. Users can also reset or delete the Android advertising identifier through device settings. Choosing not to consent to personalized advertising does not prevent the use of contextual or limited ads where permitted.

Retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is reset, cleared, or removed with the app, subject to device and operating-system behavior. Information received by Dolphin Group is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, security, support, and operational needs. Third-party providers retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Dolphin Group and its providers may process information in Canada, the United States, and other countries whose privacy laws may differ from those where a user lives.

Children

The Services are intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.
